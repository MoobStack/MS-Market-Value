This normalized dataset is an adaptation: records are grouped by numeric source/item ID,
counts are preserved and percentages are computed from those counts. The adaptation
is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Compilation tool code is separate.

Counts describe reported loot windows, not verified server probabilities or unique kills.
The public rollup does not currently distinguish kills, skinning and gathering.
Unspecified NPC/object modes stay marked npc/object in the base dataset and are not
silently assigned to a creature or gathering tooltip. Missing sources remain unknown.
No comments, screenshots, icons, site artwork or individual player records are included.
