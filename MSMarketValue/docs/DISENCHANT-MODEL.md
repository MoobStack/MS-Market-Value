# Disenchant estimates

Disenchant values use a Classic reference, unverified for Forever. Supported uncommon, rare and epic weapons/armor are matched by native item level, quality and equipment type. Unsupported items remain unavailable.

The estimate adds each possible material's chance times its average quantity times the unit price from **Price settings**. A known stack quantity scales the result. It needs a price for every possible material.

This is an expected value, not a guaranteed yield or skill-eligibility check. Fees, deposits and sale probability are excluded. Use item exclusions for known exceptions. The addon never casts Disenchant.
