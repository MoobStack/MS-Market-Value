# Release check

For MS Market Value 1.3.0, confirm these behaviors in the supported client:

- Install or update without clearing SavedVariables; verify prices, history and player records remain.
- Complete a scan, pause/resume it and inspect saved tooltip prices after reload.
- Search and change rarity/supply filters repeatedly; results settle and exact modifiers open the correct Buy listings.
- Check a recipe, vendor material cost, quote, commission and ledger export.
- Compare farming session/recent rates, idle and manual pause, combat resume and the quest-free peak.
- Change drop sort and rarity, keep a needed quest item first, inspect a personal gap and confirm no duplicate tooltip sections.
- Check window overlap, display width/lock, combat read-only views and saved preferences.

Include diagnostics and a short reproduction when reporting a failure.
