# MS Market Value 1.3.3

November 21, 2026 · WoW Forever 1.60.1 · Interface 16001

- Faction scans feed prices, history and watchlists. The addon keeps the latest neutral scan separately. Full scans share a 15-minute account cooldown, and a scan with no reply can be retried after it ends.
- On your first visit to **MS Opportunities** at a neutral auctioneer each login, **Arbitrage** opens automatically. Switch **Basis** between Daily and Intraday to compare the matching faction average with the latest neutral scan.
- Open Buy listings from current Opportunities rows while item details refresh or a paused scan waits for its first response. Every Arbitrage row opens the item’s neutral listings.
- An asterisk beside a tooltip price means **Any variant**. A different stat roll can supply that price.

Keep your SavedVariables when updating. Older prices are retained and may include scans made at neutral auctioneers before 1.3.3.
