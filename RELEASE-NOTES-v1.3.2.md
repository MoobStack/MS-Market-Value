# MS Market Value 1.3.2

November 21, 2026 · WoW Forever 1.60.1 · Interface 16001

Auction House scans work when the client hides realm names. If the addon asks for a ruleset, choose the one that character plays on. You can change it later from **Price snapshot**.

- **Daily and Intraday prices** Pick a price average under **Price settings**. At the UTC date change, the addon saves the day's averages and clears the individual price samples.
- **Market opportunities** Choose a separate price basis and sort Rising or Falling by movement, return or net profit.
- **Quicker access** Click the gold minimap button to open the addon, or drag it to move it. The scan progress bar fills the width of the Price snapshot panel.

Replace the `MSMarketValue` addon folder with the one in the Clean ZIP and keep your SavedVariables.
